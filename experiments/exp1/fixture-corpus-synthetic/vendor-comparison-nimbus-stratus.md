# Vendor comparison: Nimbus vs Stratus (hosting)

Prepared for the Q3 planning meeting. Numbers come from cost-model.csv.

| | Nimbus | Stratus |
|---|---|---|
| monthly base | 240 | 180 |
| egress | 0.02/GB | 0.09/GB |
| support | included | extra |
| regions | 4 | 11 |

Notes
- Nimbus is cheaper for us at current traffic (see the egress column).
- Stratus wins on regions, which matters if the pilot goes multi-country.
- Security review for Nimbus is in research/vendor-security-review.md and it
  is NOT finished. Do not sign anything before it is.
