## GOAL

`UD-016` fixes the performance objective: reduce content reading and hashing during
re-observation to O(changes), without claiming O(changes) for filesystem traversal.
`UD-021` bounds verdict preservation by the evidence each observation actually holds.
`UD-023` adds the historical guarantees that any implementation must preserve.

The version must therefore avoid unnecessary content reads without turning metadata into content
evidence, losing material acquisition history, or silently rewriting historical emissions.

## DEVELOPMENT STATE

The branch and CI path already exist. This record activates v0.2 as the current development line.
The first phase is **contract integration**:

1. record the approved historical guarantees in the decision log;
2. derive a version-scoped, falsifiable acceptance set from `UD-016`–`UD-027` and the P5 record;
3. obtain owner acceptance of that set;
4. only then begin test-first production changes.

No v0.2 production behaviour has changed in this phase. The v0.1 defect corrections authorized
by `UD-029` (2026-10-02) do change v0.1 behaviour; they are recorded in
[`v0.1.md`](v0.1.md) as `D-V01-6` … `D-V01-12`.

## DECIDED BOUNDARIES

- Content-read/hash work is the only O(changes) objective; traversal remains O(corpus)
  (`UD-016`).
- A skipped content read is never reported as content verification (`UD-017`).
- `ctime`, if considered, is only a heuristic and cannot establish content equality (`UD-018`).
- `basis` applicability and provenance must be determinable; their mechanisms remain open
  (`UD-019`, `UD-020`).
- Fresh reads, reused evidence and UNKNOWN/error remain distinguishable; verdict equivalence is
  bounded by the evidence actually held (`UD-021`).
- The output uses the grammar family and contract-versioning direction selected by `UD-022`;
  exact representations remain open.
- Historical emission, observational scope and coverage, material acquisition history and later
  re-evaluation obey `UD-023`.

## NON-GOALS

select a persistence schema; define retention or TTL; promise replay or physical truth; introduce
search, watcher, daemon, API, UI, MCP, AI, sync, multi-device or multi-writer behaviour; or resolve
unrelated v0.1 legibility debts.

## ACCEPTANCE GATE

The A2-1–A2-9 criteria in `V0.2-SCOPE-PROPOSAL.md` remain **PROPOSED**. Before production code:

- every accepted criterion must name an observable behaviour and a falsifier;
- criteria must reflect `UD-021` and `UD-023`, not the earlier absolute verdict-equivalence wording;
- tests must be written and observed failing before the corresponding implementation;
- filesystem-dependent claims must remain separate from deterministic reconciliation properties;
- HUMAN-INDEPENDENT evidence remains required wherever a criterion claims human legibility.

Until the owner accepts the criteria, changes are limited to recording decisions, correcting stale
status documentation, preparing the version boundary and the v0.1 defect corrections of `UD-029`. This is a checkpoint, not an implicit
acceptance of the candidate scope or technical design.
