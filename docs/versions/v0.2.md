## GOAL

`UD-016` fixes the performance objective: reduce content reading and hashing during
re-observation to O(changes), without claiming O(changes) for filesystem traversal.
`UD-021` bounds verdict preservation by the evidence each observation actually holds.
`UD-023` adds the historical guarantees that any implementation must preserve.

The version must therefore avoid unnecessary content reads without turning metadata into content
evidence, losing material acquisition history, or silently rewriting historical emissions.

## DECIDED BOUNDARIES

- Content-read/hash work is the only O(changes) objective; traversal remains O(corpus)
  (`UD-016`).
- A skipped content read is never reported as content verification (`UD-017`).
- `ctime`, if considered, is only a heuristic and cannot establish content equality (`UD-018`).
- `basis` applicability and provenance must be determinable; their mechanisms remain open
  (`UD-019`, `UD-020`).
- Fresh reads, reused evidence and UNKNOWN/error remain distinguishable; verdict equivalence is
  bounded by the evidence actually held (`UD-021`).
- The output uses the grammar family and contract-versioning direction selected by `UD-022`;
  exact representations remain open.
- Historical emission, observational scope and coverage, material acquisition history and later
  re-evaluation obey `UD-023`.
