## 4. Left open on purpose

- **Overwriting rename.** When `c` is renamed over an existing `a`, the output reports
  `renamed-or-moved path=a old-path=c` and also `deleted path=a`, although `a` still exists.
  That is V0's behaviour, kept on the owner's instruction rather than replaced by an invented
  verdict: the exact conditions for `renamed-or-moved`, `recreated` and `deleted` are an open
  semantic question. Recorded in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.
- `fsp-check/`'s own test targets carry two dead-code warnings under current clippy. V0 is
  frozen; they are recorded, not fixed.
